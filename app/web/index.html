Steam Link speaker

Plays music from Music Assistant through your Steam Link's HDMI, to the TV or receiver it's plugged into.

1 Find your Steam Link

    Not found? Turn on SSH first
    1. On a USB stick (FAT32), create the folders steamlink\config\system.
    2. In that folder, create an empty file named enable_ssh.txt.
    3. Plug the stick into the Steam Link and unplug its power for a moment to restart it.
    4. Search again. Devices named steamlink-… are listed first.

    2 Sign in

    The Steam Link's SSH password. It's steamlink123 unless you changed it.

    3 Choose how it plays

    The Steam Link connects to this address and reconnects if Music Assistant restarts.

    Ready.

    Manage

    "Use as Steam Link again" keeps the player installed but boots the normal Steam Link interface. "Remove" restores the original startup completely. Both restart the Steam Link.
