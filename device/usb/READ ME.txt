Steam Link Speaker - USB version
================================

Turns an original Valve Steam Link into a Music Assistant speaker for as long as this USB
stick is plugged in. Nothing is installed on the Steam Link.

Setting up
1. Copy everything from the download onto a USB stick (FAT32), so the stick has a
   "steamlink" folder at the top.
2. Plug the stick into the Steam Link and restart the Steam Link (unplug its power for a
   moment).
3. The Steam Link starts as usual and shows its menu for about a minute; then the speaker
   takes over. It appears in Music Assistant as "Steam Link XXXX".

Going back to normal
Pull out the stick. The Steam Link menu comes back within a few seconds.

Settings
Optional: open steamlink\speaker\settings.txt in any text editor to choose a name or set
Music Assistant's address. Restart the Steam Link afterwards.

Something not working?
The speaker writes a log to steamlink\speaker\log.txt on this stick. Plug the stick into a
computer to read it. More help: https://github.com/samuelcomplex/steamlink-sendspin

Needs Music Assistant 2.10.x with "Allow legacy clients" on (the default).
